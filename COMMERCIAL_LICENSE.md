# Request a commercial license

Westwild is free for noncommercial use. To use Westwild, its rules, or its code to earn money, request a license first.

Email **support@wordblocklabs.com** with:
1. What you want to make or run (product, module, hosted service, paid sessions, print, other).
2. How you will charge, and roughly how many people it will reach.
3. Which parts you need (code, rules, setting and lore).

Small-scale uses such as a paid game master running sessions are granted free on request. Larger uses are priced case by case. No commercial use is licensed until it is agreed in writing.

*Terms are agreed case by case, in writing, between you and me.*
