# Westwild

**A free spaghetti-western tabletop RPG.** It failed on Kickstarter because it never got enough eyes, so now it's free.

## Three ways to play
1. **The built-in tabletop on the site: wordblocklabs.com/westwild.** Web-based, nothing to install. Open it in a browser and you can start in about sixty seconds. For a table, the host opens the DM session and issues a **QR code for each player slot**; each player scans theirs with their own phone or tablet to sync to their **player deck**, so everyone holds their own character. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
2. **Pencil and paper.** The rules are in `rules-and-guides/` as plain Word documents: start with `quick start guide.docx`, then the class guides, races, loot and the bestiary. Print them or read them on a screen.
3. **Your own virtual tabletop.** Copy the rules from the same files into the VTT you already use (the rules were tested in Foundry VTT, with online dice rollers). The rules text is free to read, play and use under the license below (Westwild is a living world, so republishing or adapting the rules needs permission).

Any of the three is fine. The site's version is the one we built and tuned; the files are there so you are never locked into it.

This repository holds what travels with the game: the rules and guides, the starter campaigns, the license files, and the story of why it is free.

## Why Westwild is free
Westwild is a passion project. I started writing one day, liked what I had, and kept going. It took me most of a year. It launched as a Kickstarter campaign in June 2026 and did not fund. I could not get enough eyes on it. Instead of letting it sit in a drawer, I am releasing it free to play. If you want to use it to make money, request a license (see `COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md`). Thank you to my one backer, who was never charged and who believed in it first.

## What is here
- **Rules and guides:** `rules-and-guides/`, the Word documents the site's guides are built from (quick start, classes, races, loot and travel events, bestiary, almanac, and the setting documents).
- **Starter campaigns:** two ready-to-play campaigns in `campaigns/` (Handsome Jack the Duelist of Saint Levoir, a three-part one-shot; and Get 'Em To The Buntline, a four-part story campaign). Each folder has a `README.md` and a `*_LOAD_THIS.zip` that Westwild loads as it is. `downloads/Westwild_Starter_Campaigns.zip` is the whole pack in one file.
- **The Fan Content Policy (PDF):** `Westwild_Creator_Story_License.pdf`. You may write and sell your own unofficial stories set in the world, with credit. Nothing fan-written is canon.
- **License files:** `LICENSE.md` (the at-a-glance summary), `COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md` (how to ask for a commercial license), `FAN_CONTENT_POLICY.md`, `NOTICE.md`, and the full license texts in `licenses/`.

The playable game, the manual and the tools live on the project site: **wordblocklabs.com/westwild**.

## Which license covers which files
The **rules text** (quick start guide, class guides and features, races, compendiums, loot tables, bestiary) is copyright the author and open to **play and use**: read it, play it, run games, stream your own play. Republishing, adapting, fan material and anything commercial need permission (the Westwild Rules Play-and-Use License). The **setting and lore** documents (the Almanac, the Church of the One, Balmagoth, Red Tusk, the WERO archives, McGinty's Emporium Catalog) are **all rights reserved**: free to read and play with at the table, not to republish or sell. You may write and sell your own unofficial, non-canon stories set in the world under the **Fan Content Policy** (`FAN_CONTENT_POLICY.md`): free, no royalty, credit required; you may not change or claim the world. When a file mixes both, ask. See `LICENSE.md` for the full table.

## Licensing in one paragraph
Free and source-available, not open source in the strict sense. Home games, online games with friends, and streaming your own play are all fine. Westwild is a living world, so republishing, adaptations and fan material need permission. Making money with Westwild, or anything built on it, needs a license: email support@wordblocklabs.com.
