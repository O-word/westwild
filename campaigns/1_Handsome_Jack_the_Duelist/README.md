# Campaign 1: Handsome Jack the Duelist of Saint Levoir

One-shot starter, 3 parts. Play this one first. Starts at: Buntline Trade Road.

A starter Westwild bounty job built to teach /travel, /quest, /ccstart, clue gathering, and a clean final confrontation. Start on Buntline Trade Road, run /quest there, then /ccstart when the scene tells you to. When a part ends, follow the written next step exactly.

**Summary:** A three-part starter bounty through Saint Levoir. The posse takes a contract from Mordicai, works the Pharaoh Club for the lead, then corners Handsome Jack at Saint Levoir Station for a dead-or-alive finish.

**Core objective:** Track down Handsome Jack, confirm his location, and bring the matter to a final dead-or-alive resolution.

**Pressure:** Handsome Jack is protected by local hesitation, his own swagger, and a couple of thick-necked hangers-on who can complicate the grab.

**Reward / handoff:** Each posse member earns a 100 DD bounty writ and a summons toward Capital City, setting up the next campaign handoff.

Parts: Mordicai's Post Office, The Pharaoh Club, Saint Levoir Station Throwdown.
