# Campaign 2: Get 'Em To The Buntline

Story campaign, 4 parts. Play this one second, after Handsome Jack. Starts at: Buntline Trade Road.

A four-part Westwild story campaign that starts on Buntline Trade Road, pushes into Timberland Forest, and comes back to the Buntline with proof that the river country is about to get worse. Run /quest where the part tells you to, then /ccstart when prompted. If the table needs to skip a cutscene, use /ccend.

**Summary:** The posse finds a broken wagon hit on Buntline, tracks the kidnappers into Timberland, rescues Rockie Shaw, infiltrates a Bol hideout to save Anita, then comes back to the Buntline with proof the river country is about to get worse.

**Core objective:** Rescue the captives, identify the larger Bol Band threat, and carry the survivors and the proof back to the Buntline alive.

**Pressure:** A Bol Band operation is moving bold near patrolled country, President Shaw's daughter has been taken as ransom, and the posse has one night to act before a blood sacrifice and a larger orc movement toward the river country.

**Reward / handoff:** The posse gets a clean road-end reset on the Buntline, fresh standing with Rockie Shaw, and a strong reason to head west later before the raid-level follow-up.

Parts: Broken Wagon On The Buntline, Forward Camp In Timberland, Night At The Bol Hideout, Back To The Buntline.
