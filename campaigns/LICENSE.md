# Westwild: licensing at a glance

Westwild is **free to play, download, read and enjoy**. If you want to **make money** with it, you need to **request a license**.

Westwild is **free and source-available**. It is not "open source" in the strict sense, because commercial use is reserved.

| Part | License | Free for | Needs a license for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Code (launcher, builders, VTT, decks) | PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (`licenses/PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0.md`) | Personal, hobby, educational and other noncommercial use; modifying and sharing for free | Any commercial use |
| Rules text | Westwild Rules Play-and-Use License (`licenses/Westwild-Rules-Play-And-Use.md`), copyright the author | Reading, playing, running free games, streaming your own play | Republishing, adapting or remixing it, fan material built on it, selling or monetizing it |
| Setting, lore, the Gazette, characters | All rights reserved, Anthony "Othello" Merriweather, plus the Fan Content Policy (`FAN_CONTENT_POLICY.md`) | Playing at the table; reading; writing and selling your own unofficial, non-canon stories set in the world, under the policy | Changing the world, claiming it, republishing the lore text, modules, adaptations: ask |
| Art | Not licensed under any of the above; credited artists keep their rights | Seeing it in place | Reusing it: ask the artist or owner |

## What stays free
Home games. Online games with friends. Streaming or recording your own play. Fixing bugs in the code and sending improvements. (Republishing the rules or the world, and fan or derivative works, need permission: Westwild is a living world and it stays the same for everyone.)

## What needs a license ("commercial use")
Selling the game, a copy, a derivative, or a module or adventure built on it. Running it as a paid hosted service. Building a paid product on the code. Printing it for sale. Using it to make an app or a game you charge for.

## Paid game-running
Running a paid game session is a small-scale commercial use. Ask, and a free small-scale license is granted on request, so there is a record and no trouble.

## How to ask
Email support@wordblocklabs.com with what you plan to make and how you will charge for it. Terms are agreed case by case in writing.
